# Research References: Offline LLM-Powered Cold Outreach Engine

## Core Research Areas

### 1. Large Language Models and Local Inference

#### Foundational LLM Papers
- **"Attention Is All You Need"** - Vaswani et al. (2017)
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762
  - Foundation of transformer architecture used in modern LLMs

- **"Language Models are Few-Shot Learners"** - Brown et al. (2020)
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.14165
  - GPT-3 paper demonstrating few-shot learning capabilities

- **"LLaMA: Open and Efficient Foundation Language Models"** - Touvron et al. (2023)
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.13971
  - Meta's LLaMA models for efficient local inference

- **"Mistral 7B"** - Jiang et al. (2023)
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06825
  - High-performance 7B parameter model for local deployment

#### Model Optimization and Quantization
- **"GPTQ: Accurate Post-Training Quantization for Generative Pre-trained Transformers"** - Frantar et al. (2022)
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.17323
  - Techniques for efficient local LLM inference

- **"LLM.int8(): 8-bit Matrix Multiplication for Transformers at Scale"** - Dettmers et al. (2022)
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.07339
  - Memory-efficient inference for resource-constrained environments
